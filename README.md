# PMS–ANTICIPATION

## Purpose

**PMS–ANTICIPATION** is a paper-form PMS lens for future-oriented praxis under uncertainty.

It analyses anticipation as a structural stance under non-events, delay, restraint, preparation, temporal asymmetry, and post-confirmation drift. It does not treat anticipation as prediction, control, certainty, moral privilege, or authority to intervene.

Compact boundary:

```text
anticipation ≠ prognosis
preparedness ≠ control
non-event readability ≠ proof
future orientation ≠ authority
```

## Core Thesis

Anticipation is not future-knowledge. It is a present praxis configuration that remains structurally valid only when uncertainty, non-occurrence, temporal distance, asymmetry, and self-binding are held without turning the expected future into authority.

In PMS terms, anticipation becomes legible when Δ, □, Λ, Ω, Θ, Χ, Σ, and Ψ interact under a frame that must still remain reversible.

---

## Validity Gate

Any application use of PMS–ANTICIPATION presupposes:

| Gate | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Χ / Distance | Maintained meta-position and stop-capability; no fusion into verdict, urgency, ideology, or role authority. |
| Reversibility | Scene-bound, revisable, configuration-specific readings; no global person labels or irreversible claims. |
| Dignity-in-Practice | No shaming, ranking, humiliation, or ontological person evaluation. |

This gate constrains application. PMS and PMS–ANTICIPATION themselves may still be criticized, rejected, or ignored without satisfying the gate.

## Anticipation vs Prognosis

| Mode | Claim posture |
| --- | --- |
| Prognosis | Treats a future state as sufficiently knowable; correspondence tends to become evaluation logic. |
| Anticipation | Carries uncertainty as present structure; viability must remain intact even if the expected event does not occur. |

In PMS terms:

```text
prognosis may supply content
anticipation must not receive authority from prognosis
```

---

## Methodological Restraint

PMS–ANTICIPATION does not claim monopoly over future-oriented analysis.

Rival framings may remain useful, including predictive processing, forecasting, Bayesian updating, scenario planning, precautionary ethics, phenomenological futurity, and affective accounts of the future.

The relevant question is not whether PMS defeats those framings. The relevant question is where PMS makes anticipation structurally inspectable and where other framings remain necessary.

## Model and Example Suite

The model layer contains the lens profile, application constraints, reduced signatures, operator roles, contrast frames, drift patterns, post-confirmation discipline, scope limits, and example-suite schema.

The example suite is scene-bound, operator-readable, and non-instructional. Example themes include:

```text
AI safety regimes after first success
preventive security without a clear adversary
organizations harmed by excessive prudence
coercive moralized prevention
tragic conflict misread as preventable failure
```

Examples show possible anticipatory drift. They are not validation cases.
